About Me

Welcome to my corner of the gaming world.

Brandon D. Coleman Jr. — My Gaming World

Welcome to My Gaming World! This website brings together my interests in gaming, trucking simulators, roleplay, technology, and development.

I enjoy exploring FiveM roleplay, American Truck Simulator, and Euro Truck Simulator 2. These games offer different experiences, from community-driven roleplay to realistic virtual trucking.

I'm also interested in learning how technology works behind the scenes. Working with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and scripts helps me develop my skills and understand how websites and interactive experiences are created.

This website is a work in progress and a place where I can keep learning, share my interests, and improve my projects one step at a time.
